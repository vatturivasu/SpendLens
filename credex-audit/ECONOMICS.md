# Unit Economics

## What a converted lead is worth to Credex

Credex sells discounted AI infrastructure credits. Assume:
- Average deal: $5,000 in credits purchased (conservative for a 10-50 person startup)
- Credex margin: 15-20% on resold credits
- Revenue per closed deal: $750-$1,000
- Customer LTV (repurchase rate ~40% at 6 months): $750 x 1.4 = ~$1,050

A converted lead is worth approximately **$1,000 in revenue to Credex**.

## CAC at each channel

| Channel | Effort | Est. users/week | CAC (time cost at $50/hr) |
|---------|--------|-----------------|--------------------------|
| HN Show HN post | 2 hrs | 50-200 | $1-4/user |
| Twitter thread | 1 hr | 20-50 | $2-5/user |
| Slack group posts | 1 hr | 10-20 | $5-10/user |
| Reddit posts | 1 hr | 10-30 | $3-7/user |
| Direct LinkedIn DMs | 3 hrs | 5-10 | $15-30/user |

Blended CAC across channels: ~$5-10 per completed audit.

## Conversion funnel

| Stage | Rate | Notes |
|-------|------|-------|
| Landing page visit → audit start | 35% | Based on similar free tool benchmarks |
| Audit start → completion | 70% | Form is short, no login required |
| Completion → email capture | 20% | Value shown before gate |
| Email capture → consultation booked | 5% | Only high-savings cases see Credex CTA |
| Consultation → credit purchase | 30% | Warm lead, real savings demonstrated |

Overall: 1,000 visitors → 350 audits → 70 emails → 3-4 consultations → 1 purchase

At $1,000 revenue per purchase and $5-10 CAC per audit:
- Revenue per 1,000 visitors: ~$1,000
- Cost per 1,000 visitors: ~$5,000 (time cost)
- **Needs volume to work — the tool must go viral via shareable URLs**

## What makes this profitable

The shareable URL is the key unlock. If 10% of completed audits get shared, and each
share drives 3 new visitors, the effective CAC drops from $10 to $3. At $3 CAC and
$1,000 LTV, this is a 333x return on distribution effort.

Break-even: 5 closed deals/month covers a full-time person working on growth.

## Path to $1M ARR in 18 months

What would have to be true:

1. **1,000 audits/month by month 6** — achievable with one viral HN post + word of mouth
2. **200 email captures/month** — 20% capture rate on 1,000 audits
3. **10 consultations/month** — 5% of email captures
4. **3 closed deals/month** — 30% close rate on consultations
5. **$1,000 average revenue/deal** — conservative; enterprise deals could be $5,000+
6. **Monthly revenue at month 6:** $3,000
7. **Growth to $83k MRR by month 18:** requires either higher ACV or volume 25x current

Realistic path to $1M ARR:
- Raise ACV to $5,000 by targeting larger teams (50-200 people)
- Or drive 10,000 audits/month through SEO + partnerships
- Or both: 3,000 audits/month at $30 average revenue/audit = $90k MRR

The tool itself is a top-of-funnel asset. $1M ARR comes from Credex's sales motion
converting leads — not from the tool charging users directly.
