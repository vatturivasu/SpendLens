## FAQ

**Q: Is this actually free?**
A: Yes. No credit card, no login, no trial. SpendLens is a free tool.

**Q: Do you store my spend data?**
A: We store your tool selections and spend numbers to generate your shareable report URL. We never sell this data.

**Q: How accurate is the audit?**
A: The audit engine uses current official pricing from each vendor's pricing page, verified weekly. The recommendations are based on plan fit for your team size and use case — not guesswork.

**Q: What is Credex?**
A: Credex sources discounted AI infrastructure credits from companies that overforecast their AI usage. For teams with significant savings opportunities, Credex can help capture even more savings beyond plan downgrades.

**Q: What tools do you support?**
A: Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Claude, ChatGPT, Anthropic API, OpenAI API, Gemini, and Windsurf. More coming soon.
